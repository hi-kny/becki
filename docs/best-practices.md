# Best Practices: One-Page Site for a Calligraphy & Engraving Studio

Drawn from the two inspiration sites, the current Wellspring Writes site, and calligrapher / live-engraving vendor pages.

## What the current site is missing

The current site has About / Portfolio / Contact pages but:
- no headline saying what she does and for whom
- no visible call to action on the homepage
- no location or service area
- no testimonials or named clients
- no email or form on the homepage
- a shop cart icon with nothing in it

A one-page site fixes most of this by putting everything in one scroll with one clear action.

## Recommended page outline

1. **Hero** — logo, one-line promise, one button ("Inquire"). Large texture photo behind or beside it (`06-handmade-paper-vows-floral` or `13-wax-seals-gold-navy-envelopes`).
2. **The philosophy** — 2–3 sentences of Becky's "made for beauty, slowness, rest" message. This is what makes her different. Keep it short.
3. **Services, split by need** (the TJ Calligraphy pattern):
   - *Weddings & Celebrations* — invitations, envelope addressing, place cards, vows
   - *Live Events & Brand Activations* — on-site calligraphy and engraving for guests
   - *Gifts & Keepsakes* — engraved bottles, foil cards, personalized pieces
   Each block: one photo, two sentences, and a link to the inquiry form.
4. **Portfolio grid** — 9–12 of the strongest images, mixed shapes, large enough to see the lettering. Click to enlarge.
5. **Credibility strip** — "12 years · private clients & large-scale events · work shipped worldwide," plus client names or logos if allowed.
6. **Testimonials** — 2–3 short quotes.
7. **About Becky** — photo of her at work + the "double take" story.
8. **How it works / FAQ** — booking lead time, turnaround, travel, starting prices or minimums, what she provides vs. what the client provides.
9. **Inquiry form** — short, at the bottom, with the same button repeated in the header.
10. **Footer** — email, Instagram, Pinterest, service area, ©.

## Inquiry form fields

Keep it to what she needs to quote:
- Name, email
- What are you planning? (Wedding · Live event / brand activation · Gift / keepsake · Other)
- Event date and location
- Approximate quantity (guests, envelopes, bottles)
- For live events: hours, guest count, what items get personalized, who supplies them
- Budget range (optional, but it filters well)
- Anything else / links to inspiration

## Content & trust

- **Show finished work big.** Calligraphy is about fine detail; small thumbnails undersell it.
- **Name the service area.** Event planners search by city ("calligrapher in [city]"), and it's the first thing they check.
- **State lead time.** Wedding calligraphers often book months out; saying so sets expectations and creates urgency.
- **Give a price anchor** ("Live engraving from $___", "Envelope addressing from $___ each"). This cuts unqualified inquiries.
- **Use testimonials and named clients** for social proof, especially for brand work.
- **Show live work.** Brands and planners want to see her working at an event, not just finished pieces.

## Design

- **Let the lettering be the type star.** Use her logo/monogram as the only script; set headings in an elegant serif and body copy in a clean, readable face.
- **Palette from the photos:** warm white / cream, ink black, deep navy, antique gold accent.
- **Generous whitespace and a slow pace** that matches the "beauty, slowness, rest" message. Subtle fade-ins at most, no busy animation.
- **Mobile first.** Most Instagram and Pinterest referrals land on phones.

## Technical basics

- Compress images (WebP, ~1600px max) and lazy-load the gallery so the page stays fast.
- Write descriptive alt text for every image (it helps SEO and accessibility), e.g. "Gold foil 'Thank You' calligraphy on a cream card."
- Page title and meta description that name the service and city, e.g. "Wellspring Writes | Calligraphy & Engraving in [City]."
- Add local business schema and a Google Business Profile for local search.
- Keep good contrast. Light gold on cream fails, so use gold only for accents.
