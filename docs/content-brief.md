## Becki's message (from the doc)

> Even in the fast-paced craze of daily life, with the vast accessibility of technology and speed, we are also made for beauty, slowness, rest, and the art of learning.

Key ideas to carry into the copy:
1. **The double take.** People stop, lean in, and are in awe of an "ordinary" word or name transformed by hand.
2. **Stories.** Calligraphy prompts memories (a grandmother's penmanship, a high-school class), which points to the creative force in all of us.
3. **Credibility.** 12 years professionally, work flown to other countries, private clients and large-scale events.
4. **Who it's for.** Someone who wants to *leave an impression*. A hand-lettered piece signals intention, a curating mind, an appreciation for expression, maybe a desire to be a patron of the arts.

Tone: warm, unhurried, reverent about craft. Not salesy.
