Why by hand

Even in the fast-paced craze of what we are living through daily, with the vast accessibility of technology and speed, we are also made for beauty, slowness, rest, and the art of learning.

Most people see calligraphy and do a second take, stop, lean in, and find themselves in awe of something so “ordinary” as a word or a name, transformed almost by magic. Stories are shared of grandmothers or aunts with excellent penmanship, others about taking a calligraphy class in high school, a few folks even attempting a go at it themselves. All of it points to the creative force behind humanity and the stories we tell ourselves.

Meet the hand behind the pen

Hi, I'm Becky Lee.

I love this art form and the many shapes it has taken through the 12 years I’ve been doing this professionally. I’ve had the privilege of serving many clients, my work has been flown to different countries, and I’m still grateful to work for private clientele as well as large-scale events.

My obsession started early: origami, Korean stationery sets, and a fascination with how handwriting carries weight. A single calligraphy workshop turned it into a practice rooted in precision, artistry and presence.

What I do is for someone who wants to leave an impression. A well-executed piece of custom calligraphy, done by hand, is a bold statement. It represents intention, a curating mind, an appreciation for expression, and perhaps a desire to be a patron of the arts.
