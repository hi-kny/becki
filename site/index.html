Meet the hand behind the pen

Hi, I'm Becky Lee.

My love of paper started early: origami, Korean stationery sets, and a fascination with how much a handwritten word can carry. A single calligraphy workshop turned that love into a practice built on precision, artistry and presence.

Twelve years later, I still can’t believe this is my work. I’ve lettered for private clients and large-scale events alike, and my pieces have traveled to countries I’ve yet to visit myself.

My work is for people who want to leave an impression. Something lettered by hand says you chose with intention, that you value expression, and that you believe beautiful things are still worth making.
