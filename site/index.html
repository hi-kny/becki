Why by hand

In a world built for speed, we are also made for beauty, slowness, rest, and the art of learning.

People see calligraphy and do a double take. They stop, lean in, and find themselves in awe of something as ordinary as a name, transformed by hand. Often a story follows: a grandmother's handwriting, a class taken years ago. A hand-lettered piece says you chose with intention.

Meet the hand behind the pen

Hi, I'm Becki.

My obsession started early: origami, Korean stationery sets, and a fascination with how handwriting carries weight. A single calligraphy workshop turned it into a practice rooted in precision, artistry and presence.

Twelve years later I letter for global brands and private clients alike, and my work has traveled to other countries. Whether it's a hand-engraved gift, a live activation or a bespoke commission, every piece is made to make someone stop and lean in.
